Settings.

Manage your Insider experience, account, notifications, privacy and accessibility.

Appearance

Changes here are automatically used across the Insider pages.

Manage your basic account and career preferences.

Notifications

Choose which updates you want to receive.

Interview reminders Receive reminders before interview sessions.
Job recommendations Receive job opportunities based on your preferences.
Application updates Receive updates about applications.
Email notifications Allow important account notifications by email.

Privacy

Control profile visibility and saved activity.

Profile visibility Allow your professional profile to be discoverable.
Save interview history Keep previous interview sessions and performance results.
Personalized recommendations Use your activity to improve recommendations.

Accessibility

Adjust the interface for easier use. These changes apply across pages.

Reduce animations Reduce transitions and animations throughout Insider.
Larger text Increase interface text size.
High contrast Increase contrast on cards, borders and navigation.
